    About this journal

    I'm a weekend mountaineer and lifelong rock hound. Yunnan, in southwest China, packs glaciers, red-rock cliffs, marble ridges and sacred peaks into one province — so it became my playground. For every climb I bring back a few small stones (only where it's legal and respectful to do so) and a story. This site is where the two collide.
